But how does it
actually work?

It runs on NexSuite, an engine already in continuous government use for more than twenty years.

This is not software that was built once and left to age. For two decades it has been shaped by the ideas of government officials across all fifty US states and the time they invested to test and validate it. Every enhancement across all four One Health domains had to earn its place in real operations before it became part of the OACI Platform and the supporting technology.

As Africa inherits this foundation, the OACI Platform adapts and builds on it to meet the unique One Health collaboration needs of each country throughout the continent.

CO-FUNDED BY US FEDERAL AGENCIES

The same framework is co-funded by the FDA, USDA and Homeland Security as the coordination system for all fifty US states and their public health, agriculture and emergency response agencies.

LABORATORY NETWORK INTEGRATION

Its laboratory network integration connects the separate US networks for animal health, human clinical and public health, food and environmental testing through the Integrated Consortium of Laboratory Networks (ICLN), so an animal signal reaches the human response without either network surrendering its own autonomy.

The framework is proven in the United States. The deployment is Africa's, and each nation's governance and data remain its own.
