Ministry & Government Partners

Ministries of Health, Agriculture, and Environment form the backbone of OACI's governance. Partner ministries gain access to the full OACI Platform — including the seven integrated solutions, real-time surveillance dashboards, cross-border data sharing protocols, and capacity-building programmes tailored to national priorities. Membership includes a seat on the OACI Steering Committee and priority access to technical assistance missions.

Full OACI Platform access for all national agencies
Seat on the OACI Steering Committee
Priority technical assistance and capacity-building missions
Cross-border surveillance and data-sharing protocols

Laboratory & Research Partners

OACI partners with national reference laboratories, university research centres, and regional diagnostic networks, providing the digital infrastructure for laboratories to collaborate on building quality and capacity continent-wide. Through the OACI Platform, laboratories work together to harmonise diagnostic protocols and SOPs, coordinate proficiency testing and certification pathways, and share training programmes and methods across borders. OACI facilitates this collaboration — the science, the data, and the expertise remain with the laboratories themselves.

Collaborative platform to coordinate proficiency testing and quality assurance across partner labs
Shared workspace for harmonising SOPs, methods, and diagnostic protocols
Coordinated training and certification pathways to build laboratory capacity
Peer network for cross-border methods exchange and best practice sharing

Development & Multilateral Partners

Development partners align their One Health investments with the OACI Platform to reduce duplication, accelerate adoption, and ensure long-term sustainability. OACI provides a ready-made digital infrastructure that multilaterals can leverage for programme delivery, monitoring and evaluation, and impact assessment. Partners receive quarterly strategic briefings and co-design rights on new platform modules.

Co-design rights on new OACI Platform modules
Quarterly strategic briefings and impact dashboards
Alignment of investments with AU Member State priorities
Shared monitoring, evaluation, and learning framework

Funding & Philanthropic Partners

Funding partners power OACI's mission by supporting platform operations, country onboarding, and innovation sprints. OACI offers transparent financial reporting, real-time impact dashboards, and named recognition across all platform communications. Partners can direct funds toward specific solutions, geographic regions, or cross-cutting capabilities such as genomic surveillance or climate-health analytics.

Real-time impact dashboards and transparent reporting
Named recognition across OACI communications
Ability to direct funding toward specific solutions or regions
Annual impact report co-branded with your organisation

Technology & Innovation Partners

Technology partners integrate their tools — AI models, laboratory information systems, geospatial platforms, or mobile data collection apps — with the OACI Platform through standardised APIs. This creates a continent-scale marketplace for One Health technology while ensuring interoperability with national systems. Partners retain full IP ownership and gain access to OACI's user base across 55 member states.

API integration with the OACI Platform at continent scale
Access to OACI's user base across 55 member states
Full IP retention for your technology
Co-marketing and joint conference presence

PARTNERSHIP PATHWAY
Implementation & Field Partners

Implementation partners are the front line of One Health delivery — conducting field surveillance, training community health workers, and translating platform data into local action. OACI provides these partners with mobile-ready tools, offline-capable data collection, and direct integration with national dashboards. Field partners also contribute real-world feedback that shapes the platform roadmap.

Mobile-ready, offline-capable data collection tools
Direct integration with national One Health dashboards
Training and certification for community health workers
Input into the OACI Platform roadmap and feature prioritisation
